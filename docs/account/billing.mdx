Billing lives under **Account > Billing and Usage**. Two things are billed
separately:

- the **platform subscription** — the plan you choose, billed monthly or
  annually through Stripe
- **hosted model usage** — token consumption for scans that run on
  ZeroQuarry-managed LLMs, itemized per model and invoiced monthly on top of
  the subscription

Calls made with your own provider key are billed by that provider, not by
ZeroQuarry; they appear in the usage table as "Your provider" but are not
invoiced by ZeroQuarry.

![ZeroQuarry Billing and Usage page with plans, payment method, and current usage.](/img/screenshots/billing-usage.png)

_The Billing and Usage page carries the plan checkout, payment method, and
the current month's usage. Past invoices appear here once hosted model usage
has been invoiced._

## Choose a plan

Available plans are listed with their monthly and annual prices. Changing
plans goes through Stripe Checkout; the change applies to the account as a
whole, not to individual members.

## Payment method

Payment methods are managed in the Stripe customer portal. Use **Manage
payment methods** to update the card on file or review invoices in Stripe.
Invoices and receipts are emailed to the account's billing contacts.

## Monthly model usage

Plan capacity is metered in **security runs**: a focused PR or changed-file
review uses one run, and a full source, binary, or remote assessment uses
five. Reports, decisions, sharing, and pipeline-stage re-runs do not consume
additional runs.

On top of the plan, the usage table shows the current month's token
consumption per model — input, cache-hit, and output tokens — split between
ZeroQuarry-hosted models and calls funded by your own provider key. Hosted
usage accrues to a monthly invoice per model; if a month's invoice could not
be issued at the time (for example, before a payment method was on file), it
is caught up automatically once billing is configured.

## The billing role

Teammates who need to manage payment details or receive invoices do not need
full admin access. Invite them with the **billing** role under **Account >
Members**; billing-role members can manage the Stripe portal session and
billing contacts but cannot create scans or change security settings.

Billing contacts default to the account owner. Invite a billing teammate and
have them set the contact to change where invoices are delivered.

## What happens if a plan expires

When a plan or trial expires, new scans and artifact generation are blocked,
but existing reports, findings, evidence, and exports remain readable. Settle
the billing issue in the Stripe portal to resume normal operation.
