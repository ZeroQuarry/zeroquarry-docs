Billing lives under **Account > Billing and Usage**. Two things are billed
separately:

- the **platform subscription** — the plan you choose, billed monthly or
  annually through Stripe
- **hosted model usage** — token consumption for scans that run on
  ZeroQuarry-managed LLMs, itemized per model and invoiced monthly on top of
  the subscription

Calls made with your own provider key are billed by that provider, not by
ZeroQuarry; they appear in the usage table as "Your provider" but are not
invoiced by ZeroQuarry.

![ZeroQuarry Billing and Usage page with plans, payment method, and current usage.](/img/screenshots/billing-usage.png)

_The Billing and Usage page carries the plan checkout, payment method, and
the current month's usage. Past invoices appear here once hosted model usage
has been invoiced._

## The trial

A new account starts on a 30-day trial. No card is required. The trial
includes one pool of **6 security-run units** for the whole 30 days: the pool
does not reset partway through, and a focused review that uses one unit and a
full assessment that uses four draw from the same pool. A per-source cap
also applies to free scanning: the free allowance per repository owner (or
target host) is shared across all ZeroQuarry accounts, so signing up again
does not refill it.

When the pool is at 80% or spent, a banner appears on every page with the
numbers behind it and, for owners and admins, a link to the plans on the
billing page. New scans and reruns are refused once the pool is spent, and
the banner shows when the trial ends; the allowance does not reset
mid-trial. Calls billed to your own provider keys bypass the token caps, but
the security-run pool still applies.

## Choose a plan

The **Plans** card on the billing page lists every self-serve plan with its
monthly and annual prices. Annual billing saves 20% (for example, a plan at
$50/month is $480/year). Pick a price on a plan's row and Stripe Checkout
takes the card details; the plan applies to the account as a whole, not to
individual members.

The card header says what each part costs: "Platform subscription. Hosted
model usage is billed monthly on top; calls made with your own provider key
are billed by that provider."

### Upgrading from the trial

Upgrading ends the trial immediately. Two things are worth knowing:

- **The paid allowances start fresh at the upgrade.** Token, spend, scan,
  and security-run windows all begin at the moment the plan activates, so
  trial usage does not eat into the first paid month.
- **Trial usage is never billed automatically.** The next renewal is the
  normal plan price. Model usage from the trial sits in its own ledger that
  the monthly invoicer never reads, and upgrading does not sweep it onto an
  invoice.

## Change or cancel a plan

Once the account has a subscription, the billing page shows a **Your
subscription** card instead of the plan list, so a second subscription
cannot be bought by accident. That card is where plan changes and
cancellation live.

### Change plan

Choose **Change plan** (owner or admin). The picker lists your current plan
and higher-priced plans, plus the other billing period for your own tier;
moving to a lower-priced plan is not available self-serve, and the picker
says to contact us instead.

Plan changes take effect at your next renewal, not immediately. Nothing is
charged on the day you schedule the change; the card on file is charged the
new plan's price when it takes over, and usage allowances reset to the new
plan's limits at that boundary. Work already done stays where it is. You can
undo a scheduled change any time before the renewal date, and re-aiming the
picker replaces the previous switch rather than stacking a second one.

A scheduled cancellation outranks a scheduled switch: undo the cancellation
first if you also want to change plan.

### Cancel

Choose **Cancel subscription** (owner or admin). The confirmation page sets
out exactly what happens:

- **Until the period ends:** nothing changes. The plan stays fully active,
  because it is already paid through that date.
- **On the end date:** one final bill for that month's hosted-model usage,
  charged to the card on file. A balance under the $0.50 invoicing minimum
  is skipped entirely.
- **After the end date:** the account becomes read-only. Reports remain
  available to download while the account winds down, and it is deleted 60
  days after the subscription ends. Deletion is permanent.
- **Any time before the end date:** undo the cancellation from the billing
  page and nothing changes at all.

While a cancellation is scheduled, the subscription card shows the date the
plan ends and an **Undo cancellation** button.

During wind-down the account keeps its reports: scans cannot run and members
cannot sign in, but the owner can browse, export, and download, and can
choose **Cancel deletion and re-activate** to resume the subscription before
the 60 days are up.

## Payment method

The **Payment** card shows whether a card is actually on file: a green
**payment method on file** badge when it is, with **Manage payment methods
(Stripe)** next to it for updating the card or reviewing invoices. Invoices
and receipts are emailed to the account's billing contacts, and the card
names the address they are sent to.

An account with no payment method and no subscription generates no usage
invoices. Abandoning a checkout leaves nothing owed and no stranded draft
invoice behind.

## Plan usage: security runs

Plan capacity is metered in **security runs**. A scan reserves against the
monthly allowance at launch — five for a full source, binary, or remote
assessment, one for a focused changed-file review or a verification-only retest
— and settles when it finishes: one run per pipeline stage that completed
(typically four for a full assessment), one for a focused product, and zero for
a run that failed or delivered nothing.

The usage card shows the settled charge for the current month plus the full
reservation of any scan still in flight. That is why a five-run launch can show
the month higher than the runs it has actually cost so far. Reports, decisions,
sharing, and pipeline-stage re-runs consume no additional runs, and deleting a
finished scan does not return its runs.

The account overview always shows the security-run row with a used/limit bar
and the reset date, so the "how many runs do I have left" number does not
require a trip to the billing page. Token and platform-spend rows appear
there once they reach 80% of their cap. When a scan is refused for lack of
runs, the refusal page links back to the plan and the usage breakdown.

See [How security runs are counted](/account/security-runs) for the stage
breakdown, the settlement rules for failed and cancelled scans, and the
per-source allowance on free and trial plans.

### Hosted model usage

On top of the plan, the usage table shows the current month's token
consumption per model — input, cache-hit, and output tokens — split between
ZeroQuarry-hosted models and calls funded by your own provider key. Hosted
usage accrues to a monthly invoice per model; if a month's invoice could not
be issued at the time (for example, before a payment method was on file), it
is caught up automatically once billing is configured.

Security runs and model usage are separate meters. A refunded run still burned
tokens, and those tokens still appear in this table.

## The billing role

Teammates who need to manage payment details or receive invoices do not need
full admin access. Invite them with the **billing** role under **Account >
Members**. A billing member can manage payment methods and billing contacts
and can launch self-serve scans against the plan's security-run allowance,
but cannot manage members, integrations, or account settings.

Billing contacts default to the account owner. Invite a billing teammate and
have them set the contact to change where invoices are delivered.

## What happens if a plan expires

An expired trial refuses new scans and artifact generation with "Your trial
has expired. Choose a plan to continue." Existing reports, findings,
evidence, and exports remain readable.

A paid plan that reaches its end date without a renewal enters the same
wind-down described under [Cancel](#cancel): the account is read-only, one
final usage bill is issued, and the owner can re-activate before deletion.
